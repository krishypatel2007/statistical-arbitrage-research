\# Statistical Arbitrage Research



\## Objective

To design and evaluate a statistically robust mean-reversion trading strategy

based on cointegration and stochastic modelling.



\## Research Questions

\- Can cointegrated equity pairs be identified reliably?

\- Do their spreads exhibit stable mean-reversion?

\- Can this behaviour be exploited after costs?



\## Scope

\- Daily equity data

\- Long/short market-neutral strategies

\- Focus on statistical validity over raw performance
